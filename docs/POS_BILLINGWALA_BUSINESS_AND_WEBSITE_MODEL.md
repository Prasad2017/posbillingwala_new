## 2. Overall Business Model

### 2.1 Three Pillars + Distribution

| Pillar | What You Sell | Revenue Type |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| **Software (SaaS)** | POS Billing subscription & renewal | Recurring |
| **Hardware** | POS machine, Bluetooth/thermal printer | One-time |
| **Consumables** | 57mm & 80mm billing rolls, labels, ribbons | Repeat purchase |
| **Services** | Installation, training, remote support, menu setup | Included or add-on |
| **Distribution** | Regional & area dealer network | Margin sharing |

### 2.2 Business Flow

```text
POS Billingwala (Head Office / Company)
        │
        ├── Regional Dealers
        │       │
        │       ├── Area Dealers
        │       │       │
        │       │       ├── Sales & Marketing Team
        │       │       ├── Customer Support Team
        │       │       └── Installation / Technical Team
        │       │
        │       └── End Customers (Hotels, Shops, Mess, Retail, etc.)
        │
        ├── Software Development & Updates
        ├── License & Subscription Management
        └── Brand, Pricing Policy, Legal & Compliance
```

### 2.3 Why This Model Works in India

- Customers trust **local dealer + phone/WhatsApp support** more than pure online SaaS.
- Hardware (printer, rolls, POS machine) creates **sticky, repeat business**.
- Subscription + renewal creates **predictable recurring revenue**.
- Dealer network enables **city-by-city expansion** without opening offices everywhere.

### 2.4 Comparable Positioning

This model aligns with successful Indian POS/billing companies that combine:

- Software subscription
- Local partner/dealer network
- Installation and ongoing support
- Optional hardware sales

POS Billingwala should present as a **complete billing solution provider**, not only an app download.

## 3. Company Hierarchy

### 3.1 Organizational Structure

```text
POS Billingwala → Company / Head Office
    → Regional Dealers
        → Area Dealers
            → Sales & Marketing Team
            → Customer Support Team
            → Installation / Technical Team
                → Customers
```

### 3.2 Role Definitions

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Head Office** | Product, brand, pricing policy, license system, legal, central support escalation |
| **Regional Dealer** | Multi-city/district oversight, dealer onboarding, sales targets |
| **Area Dealer** | Local sales, installation, first-line support, renewal collection |
| **Sales & Marketing** | Demos, leads, customer onboarding, upsell (printer, rolls) |
| **Customer Support** | Phone/WhatsApp, remote help, renewal reminders, ticket handling |
| **Technical Team** | Printer setup, data sync, menu/product setup, software training |
| **Customer** | Uses POS app, renews license, buys consumables via dealer |

### 3.3 Dealer Network Benefits

**For company:**
- Scales without heavy local office cost
- Local market knowledge via dealers
- Faster installation and support response

**For dealers:**
- Clear product to sell (software + hardware)
- Recurring renewal income
- Brand backing from head office

**For customers:**
- Known local contact
- Same-day or next-day support possible
- Trust through face-to-face relationship

## 4. Revenue Streams

### 4.1 Software Plans (Subscription & Renewal)

| Plan Type | Validity | Price (₹) | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Subscription | 6 Months | ₹ ___ | New customer — first purchase |
| Subscription | 1 Year | ₹ ___ | New customer — annual plan |
| Renewal | 6 Months | ₹ ___ | Existing customer — extend license |
| Renewal | 1 Year | ₹ ___ | Existing customer — annual renewal |

**GST display rule (mandatory on website & invoice):**

- Clearly state: **GST included** OR **GST extra (+ 18%)**
- Same wording on website pricing table and dealer quotes
- Avoid customer surprise at invoice time

**Pricing strategy tips:**

- Show **"Starting from ₹___"** on website if area-wise pricing varies
- Offer **renewal discount** vs new subscription (optional)
- Highlight **1 Year = best value** for retention

Trust sections are **not optional** for a professional B2B SaaS + hardware business.

### 7.1 Footer — Mandatory Fields

| Item | Example placeholder |
|------|---------------------|
| Legal company name | [Registered Pvt Ltd / LLP name] |
| GSTIN | 27XXXXX1234X1ZX |
| Registered office | Full address, city, state, PIN |
| Official support phone | +91 XXXXXXXXXX |
| Official email | support@posbillingwala.com |
| Business hours | Mon–Sat, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM |

### 7.2 Required Policy Pages

| Policy | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Privacy Policy** | Data collection, storage, third-party SDKs — Play Store requirement |
| **Terms & Conditions** | License usage, account rules, liability limits |
| **Refund Policy** | When refund is/is not applicable (software + hardware) |
| **Renewal Policy** | How renewal works, grace period, data after expiry |

### 7.3 Refund / Renewal Policy — Key Points to Define

**Renewal:**
- License validity period
- Grace period after expiry (if any)
- What happens to data when license expires
- How to renew (dealer, online, phone)
- Price difference: new subscription vs renewal

**Refund:**
- Software: typically no refund after activation (state clearly)
- Hardware: defective product replacement window
- Contact channel for disputes

### 7.4 Invoice & Website Consistency

Website pricing, dealer quotes, and GST invoice must all match:

- Same plan names
- Same validity periods
- Same GST treatment (included vs extra)
