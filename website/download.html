Installation guide & licence

  1. Download from Google Play or APK link (if provided by dealer).
  2. Install and open POS Billingwala on your Android device.
  3. Enter licence key provided by dealer or admin.
  4. Configure printer, categories, and products — or ask dealer for setup help.
  5. Start billing — works offline after initial setup.

Licence activation & login

  • Customer licence — enter key in POS app after purchase from dealer.
  • Owner login — use Owner app with credentials from setup.
  • Dealer login — Dealer portal (authorized dealers only).
  • Web admin — Admin login for reports and configuration.
